# Asset sources

- **Fonts** (subset to Latin, embedded as base64 WOFF2): Barlow, Barlow Condensed, JetBrains Mono, from github.com/google/fonts (ofl/). SIL Open Font License 1.1. Barlow and Barlow Condensed share one OFL text.
- **Tech icons**: Simple Icons, github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons, commit 98820a4 (2026-10-04). CC0 1.0. Brand marks remain trademarks of their owners.
- **LinkedIn and portfolio card icons**: generic briefcase and globe glyphs drawn for this profile. LinkedIn's mark is not in Simple Icons, so no brand mark is used.
- **Portraits**: id.png and right_pointing.png supplied by the profile owner.
